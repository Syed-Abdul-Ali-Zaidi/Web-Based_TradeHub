About TradeHub

TradeHub is a web-based barter platform that allows users to exchange items with one another. Our goal is to make trading simple by helping people discover useful items, propose exchanges, and give their belongings a new purpose.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a convenient platform where people can exchange goods without relying entirely on traditional buying and selling. TradeHub allows users to list their items, explore available listings, and make offers based on their needs and interests.

By encouraging the reuse of existing belongings, we aim to make exchanging items more accessible and promote more sustainable consumption habits.

What We Offer

TradeHub provides essential features to help users participate in the barter process.

Item Listings

Users can list their items with descriptions, categories, and pictures to let others know what they have available for exchange.

Flexible Offers

Users can propose exchanges involving their own items and, where supported, additional money to negotiate a suitable trade.

Offer Management

Users can review incoming offers, respond to proposals, and keep track of their ongoing trading activities.

How It Works

1. Explore Items

Browse the marketplace and discover items listed by other users.

2. Make an Offer

Select an item and propose an exchange using your own items or other supported offer options.

3. Negotiate an Exchange

Review responses to your offer and work with the other user to reach an agreement.

Support

We understand that users may have questions or encounter difficulties while using TradeHub. This section provides guidance on common issues related to the platform.

Account Issues

If you experience difficulties registering, logging in, or accessing your account, check that you have entered the correct account information.

Item Listings

Make sure your item descriptions, categories, and pictures accurately represent the items you want to exchange.

Offers and Exchanges

Review your offer details and its current status if you have questions about a proposed exchange. You can respond to offers using the options available on the platform.

If you need further assistance, please contact our support team at support@tradehub.com.
